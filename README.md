HavenStay PG — Student PG & Co-Living Hostel Website

A clean, responsive PG and hostel management webpage built using pure HTML5 and CSS3 (with zero JavaScript, back-end servers, or external frameworks).

🔗 Live Demo: https://startling-crostata-f70731.netlify.app

Features & Sections

Sticky Responsive Navigation: Includes smooth anchor scrolling across sections (Available Rooms, Facilities, Food Menu, Hostel Rules, Support Desk, Book / Contact) and a pure-CSS mobile hamburger menu (#nav-toggle).

Hero & Quick Metrics: Highlights admissions status, location details, live availability metrics (14 Beds available, 4 Meals daily, 24/7 CCTV & security), and separate boys/girls wing information.

Available Rooms & Monthly Tariff: Showcases Single Private Room (₹13,500/mo), Twin Sharing Deluxe (₹9,500/mo), and Triple Sharing Comfort (₹7,200/mo) with bed availability badges, room amenities, and reservation links.

Room & Campus Facilities: Highlights 300 Mbps fiber Wi-Fi, automatic laundry, 100% power backup, biometric & CCTV security, RO + UV drinking water, and a 24/7 reading lounge.

Food & Weekly Mess Menu: Displays daily meal timings alongside a complete 7-day (Monday – Sunday) schedule covering Breakfast, Lunch, Evening Snacks, and Dinner.

Hostel Rules & Guidelines: Outlines six clear community policies covering gate timings, quiet study hours, visitor policies, zero-tolerance rules, electrical safety, and the monthly rent/notice cycle.

Complaint & Maintenance Support Desk: Features a 24/7 escalation matrix (Maintenance, IT/Wi-Fi, Medical & Emergency Warden) alongside a ticket submission form with a pure-CSS :target confirmation modal (#ticket-submitted).

Contact Details & Room Inquiry: Provides campus address, warden helpline numbers, visiting hours, and a room booking inquiry form with a pure-CSS confirmation modal (#booking-submitted).

Tech Stack

HTML5 — Semantic structure and accessible form controls

CSS3 — Custom properties (design tokens), CSS Grid & Flexbox layouts, responsive media queries, and pure-CSS interactive states (:checked and :target)
