Community Code

Hostel Rules & Guidelines

Simple guidelines to ensure a peaceful, respectful, and secure living environment for every resident.

RULE 01

Gate Timings & Biometric Entry

Main gate closes at 10:30 PM. Late entry for academic labs or travel requires prior digital permission from the Resident Warden.

RULE 02

Quiet Study Hours

Strict silence must be maintained in corridors and rooms between 10:00 PM and 06:00 AM. Use headphones for music or calls.

RULE 03

Visitor & Parent Policy

Parents and registered guests are welcome in the Ground Floor Visitor Lounge between 09:00 AM and 07:00 PM with valid ID.

RULE 04

Zero Tolerance Policy

Smoking, alcohol, narcotics, and ragging are strictly prohibited inside the hostel premises and will lead to immediate expulsion.

RULE 05

Electrical Appliance Safety

High-wattage heating coils, electric irons, and induction stoves are not permitted inside bedrooms to prevent short circuits.

RULE 06

Notice Period & Rent Cycle

Monthly rent is due by the 5th of every month. A minimum 30-day prior notice is required for security deposit refund.
